# Third-party data

`extension/rules/*.json` and `extension/cosmetic/*` are generated by `node tools/build-rules.mjs` from the
filter lists that Brave Shields enables by default, as named in Brave's list catalog
(https://github.com/brave/adblock-resources, MPL-2.0). No list text is edited by hand;
this project's own additions live in `tools/custom-filters.txt` and are compiled in alongside.
